# PM-005 Toast AirPods Case render set

## Product lock

- One compact protective earbud charging case shaped like a slice of toast.
- Cream porous bread-crumb texture across the front and back.
- Evenly browned caramel crust around the perimeter and lid.
- Two-part shell with a working hinge, charging opening, speaker holes, and side keyring.
- Generic white wireless earbuds with no Apple logo or copyrighted UI.
- Warm off-white studio palette with soft grounded shadows.
