## Product direction

A compact collapsible phone grip shaped like a tiny slice of toast. The Butter variant is the lead design. A medium raised butter slab sits above a generous melted layer so the topping feels full without hiding the toast. A molded cream silicone top, caramel-brown crust rim, flexible two-stage accordion neck, and flat adhesive mounting disc form one practical accessory.

The grip series from the supplied toast product board was used as broad direction. Other products, labels, and layouts from that board were excluded.

## Shared visual language

- Square premium product photography
- Warm off-white studio background
- Soft diffused editorial light
- Realistic injection-molded seams and compact accessory scale
- Phone attachment aligned to the true center of the back panel
- Calm independent Korean design catalog mood
- No actual food, unrelated logos, or watermarks
