## Product direction

The lower toaster body is a desktop charging dock. The upper toast-shaped piece is a removable mini power bank. Returning the toast module to the gold-lined slot charges it, while removing it allows portable phone charging.

The existing PM-001 render was used as the strict identity reference. Its compact cream body, black inset pixel display, golden trim, tan side lever, rounded proportions, and warm off-white studio language were preserved across the new set.

## Shared visual language

- Square premium product renders for the PixelMurmur detail gallery
- Warm off-white background and soft controlled studio light
- Matte cream molded shell, toasted gold accents, and charcoal display glass
- Believable charging contacts, USB-C interfaces, seams, indicators, and cable routing
- Simple monochrome pixel display states
- Cute but refined consumer electronics, not a toy and not real food
- No product-board text, unrelated logos, watermarks, neon, or cyberpunk styling
