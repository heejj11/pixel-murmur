# PM-006 Toast Card Wallet render set

## Product lock

- One compact zip-around card wallet shaped like a slice of toast.
- Cream porous bread-crumb panel across the front.
- Evenly browned caramel crust with visible edge stitching.
- Slim gusset, fine tan zipper track, metal slider, and small leather pull.
- Practical card sleeves and one folded-note compartment.
- Plain generic cards with no numbers, logos, or branded graphics.
- Warm off-white studio palette with soft grounded shadows.
